# Specialist Profile Acceptance Checklist

A profile must not be called production-ready until it has:
- [ ] Clear best-fit and non-fit cases.
- [ ] Audience outcome and story structure guidance.
- [ ] Visual, editorial, and sound grammar.
- [ ] Asset and rights requirements.
- [ ] Production workflow and storyboard data requirements.
- [ ] P'Nata policy application.
- [ ] Objective and subjective quality gates.
- [ ] Common failure modes and prevention.
- [ ] At least one representative sample brief and review rubric.
- [ ] Clear capability dependencies and fallback methods.

## Project-level profile acceptance
- [ ] Profile selection is based on the brief, not habit or available template.
- [ ] One primary profile owns overall editorial coherence.
- [ ] Supporting profiles have explicit sequence ownership.
- [ ] Profile-specific QC is included in the final report.
- [ ] No profile-specific requirement is silently skipped because a renderer lacks support.
