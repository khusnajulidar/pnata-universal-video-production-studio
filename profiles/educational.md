# Educational / Tutorial / Explainer Profile

## Purpose and fit
Help a defined audience acquire understanding or perform a task accurately. Success is measured by what the learner can explain, recognize, decide, or do.

## Learning design
Define audience level, prerequisites, objectives, and misconceptions. Organize from known to new and concrete to abstract where useful. Break content into manageable units; one central idea per beat. Use worked examples, demonstrations, comparison, retrieval prompts, and recap when useful. Avoid cognitive overload.

## Instructional clarity
Demonstrate steps in correct order. Show relevant states before and after an action. Use consistent terminology, readable labels, warnings, and exceptions when omission could mislead.

## Religious education
Verify Qur'an, hadith, translations, scholarly attributions, and fiqh details from reliable sources. Preserve exact wording where required. Do not invent rulings or flatten legitimate scholarly nuance. Escalate unresolved religious uncertainty.

## Accessibility and QC
Use clear speech, suitable pace, contrast, legible captions where requested, and cues beyond colour alone. Objectives must be met; sequence correct; no factual/religious error; each step understandable; narration and labels agree.
