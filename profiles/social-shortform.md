# Social Short-form / Mobile-first Profile

## Purpose and fit
Deliver one clear idea or action in a platform-native, mobile-first context.

## Design
Choose aspect ratio, duration, captions, safe zones, and pacing from the target platform and brief. Do not assume every short video must be vertical or under a fixed duration. Design for silent autoplay when relevant without replacing narration with dense text.

## Narrative
Open with a specific question, tension, benefit, or surprising but accurate fact. Deliver value early. Use compact progression and one clear ending/CTA. Avoid misleading hooks, bait-and-switch, and speed that prevents comprehension.

## Mobile craft and QC
Use readable type, contrast, margins, clear focal point, and platform-safe placement. Preview at phone scale and after representative compression. Hook must be truthful; captions match speech; ending is not clipped; edit works without music.
