# Profile Selection Matrix

| Brief signal | Primary profile | Supporting profiles often useful | Primary risk to control |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mood, reflection, atmosphere, visual metaphor | Cinematic | Motion graphics, social short-form | Beautiful but empty imagery |
| Explain through animated diagrams, type, data | Motion graphics | Educational, presentation | Motion obscures meaning |
| Real events, evidence, investigation | Documentary | Cinematic, motion graphics | Misleading framing or weak sourcing |
| Teach a concept or procedure | Educational | Motion graphics, presentation | Cognitive overload or incorrect steps |
| Promote an offer, product, service, campaign | Promotional | Cinematic, motion graphics, social | Unsupported claims or vague CTA |
| Feed-first short mobile content | Social short-form | Any content profile | Hook without substance; unreadable text |
| Talk, lecture, narrated deck, structured argument | Presentation | Educational, motion graphics | Dense slides and poor narration sync |
| Distinct scene grammars with one coherent story | Hybrid | Select scene-specific profiles | Style fragmentation |

## Do not select by label alone
A "documentary-style promo" may be promotional primary with documentary evidence gates. A "cinematic explainer" may be educational primary with cinematic visual treatment. Choose based on the audience outcome and dominant storytelling vehicle.

## Selection record
For every project, record:
- primary profile and reason;
- supporting profiles and exact sequences they own;
- quality tier target;
- non-negotiable quality gates;
- execution backend per major sequence;
- known capability gaps and fallback plan.
