# Documentary / Evidence-led Storytelling Profile

## Purpose and fit
Tell factual stories with traceable evidence, fair context, transparent uncertainty, and responsible editorial treatment.

## Research
Build a claim ledger: exact claim, type, source, date, primary/secondary status, corroboration, confidence, and caveats. Prefer primary records, original documents, datasets, and first-hand material. Verify names, dates, locations, numbers, quotations, translations, and causal claims independently. Separate observed fact, allegation, interpretation, and narration. Never turn correlation into causation or absence of evidence into proof.

## Narrative and ethics
Use a clear question, stakes, evidence sequence, complication/context, and proportionate conclusion. Do not manufacture suspense that changes factual meaning or omit material counterpoints for drama. Label illustrative footage, maps, timelines, and reconstructions. Never fabricate witnesses, quotations, documents, or evidence.

## P'Nata gates
No complete living beings. No music. Only speech, reviewed non-musical SFX, genuinely non-musical ambience, or silence.

## QC
Every material claim is sourced; context is current; quotations match; illustrative visuals are not misrepresented; uncertainty is visible; edit does not imply unsupported causation.
