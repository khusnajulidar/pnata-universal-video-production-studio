# Hybrid Production Profile

## Purpose and fit
Combine multiple production grammars when the communication goal genuinely needs them—for example documentary evidence with cinematic sequences and motion-graphic explanations.

## Composition rules
Choose one primary profile to own story and editorial coherence. Assign supporting profiles to specific scenes/sequences. Define continuity across boundaries: palette, typography, camera grammar, narration, pacing, transitions, and source labelling. Maintain one claim ledger, asset manifest, timeline, and QC report.

## Scene contract
Each scene declares purpose, profile, viewer takeaway, visual method, asset provenance, narration/audio needs, duration rationale, transitions, and applicable quality gates.

## QC
The whole video feels intentional; profile shifts have narrative motivation; no contradictory visual systems; assets are traceable; all profile-specific gates pass.
