# Cinematic / Atmospheric Visual Essay Profile

## Purpose and fit
Build meaning through images, spatial relationships, light, texture, movement, temporal rhythm, and narration. Use for cinematic essays, reflective pieces, mood-led storytelling, and narrative-led films. Lead when the viewer should feel scale, tension, passage of time, or emotional consequence—not merely because a brief says "cinematic".

## Story design
Establish a visual question or tension. Build progression from orientation to pressure/contrast, discovery, consequence, and considered resolution. Let images add information or subtext; do not literally illustrate every spoken sentence. Develop recurring motifs and visual evolution.

## Visual craft
Specify framing, camera distance, perspective, depth planes, motivated movement, and light direction. Define lighting source, softness, contrast, colour temperature, atmosphere, and continuity. Use shot variety: establishing, detail, texture, abstract insert, transition image, closing image. Grade for continuity, not as a one-click look. Avoid random zooms, pans, shakes, and generic slow motion.

P'Nata restriction: no complete human or animal depiction, including silhouettes. Use architecture, landscapes, objects, weather, water, material detail, empty spaces, and abstract metaphor.

## Editorial and sound
Cut for movement, shape, sound event, contrast, or thought. Vary shot duration according to narrative function. Review pacing with voice muted and audio alone. Only speech, reviewed non-musical SFX, genuinely non-musical environmental ambience, and silence. No rhythmic SFX patterns functioning as a beat.

## QC
Every shot adds meaning; movement and lighting are motivated; grade is continuous; no complete or implied assembled living figure; narration remains clear; opening and ending are purposeful.
