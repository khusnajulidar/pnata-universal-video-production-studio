# Promotional / Product / Brand Profile

## Purpose and fit
Communicate a truthful value proposition and move a defined audience toward an appropriate action.

## Strategy
Define audience, need, positioning, differentiator, proof, objection, offer, CTA, platform, and conversion path. Make one primary promise and support it with evidence. Never invent performance claims, testimonials, certifications, scarcity, or guarantees.

## Visual and editorial craft
Prioritize the actual product/service and its use context. Establish brand consistency without sacrificing legibility. Product details, interface captures, diagrams, and typography can replace people-focused imagery. Structure: real audience need → value → proof → objection resolved → clear CTA. Avoid generic hype and false urgency.

## Rights and QC
Confirm permission for logos, product imagery, trademarks, fonts, footage, and third-party assets. Online availability is not permission. Verify offer, CTA, URL, price, product depiction, claims, comparisons, and brand usage.
