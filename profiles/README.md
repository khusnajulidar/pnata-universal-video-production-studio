# Specialized Production Profiles

P'Nata is universal in coverage, not generic in execution. The Universal Core owns shared governance and delivery; a profile supplies craft-specific methods, production grammar, specialist decisions, and quality gates.

## Profile selection
The Creative Director identifies the viewer outcome, dominant storytelling vehicle, chooses one primary profile and normally no more than two supporting profiles, records the rationale, and assigns scene ownership. Hybrid projects are assigned by sequence; do not flatten them into a generic template.

Profiles: cinematic, motion graphics, documentary, educational, promotional, social short-form, presentation, and hybrid. Profiles are methods, not presets. Never force fixed duration, aspect ratio, colour palette, pacing, or style without a brief-based reason.

## Quality doctrine
- Craft quality is judged against the selected profile and actual brief.
- A technically valid render can still fail creative, factual, accessibility, rights, or syariah review.
- Do not compensate for weak substance with visual effects.
- Preserve approved decisions and assets.
- If a specialist capability is unavailable, declare the limitation and propose an honest fallback.
